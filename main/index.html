Deck Lab
Hand feel prototype
Gestures
  • Slide across the hand to peek at cards (mouse: just hover)
  • Tap a card to select it, then tap Play above it
  • Drag up past the play line, or flick, to play
  • Drag sideways low in the hand to reorder (or long-press)
  • Tap the deck to draw · tap the pile to return played cards
  • Overflowing hand: swipe it, or use the mouse wheel / trackpad
  • Mouse works for everything: click = tap, click-drag = drag
  • Double-click a setting's label to reset it
